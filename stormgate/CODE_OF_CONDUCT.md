# Code of Conduct

Storm Gate adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, give and accept
constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling and publishing other
people's private information are not acceptable in any project space
(issues, pull requests, discussions, chat).

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/davimf721/Storm-Gate/security/advisories/new)
marked "Code of Conduct", or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and warn, suspend or
ban participants, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
